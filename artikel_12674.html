Alocity Technologies Promotes Adelaine Orpin to Chief Commercial Officer

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Leadership appointment strengthens Alocity's commercial strategy as the company accelerates growth of its AI-powered cloud security management platform.

FORT LAUDERDALE, Fla., Nov. 3, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Alocity, an innovator in AI-powered physical security and cloud-based security management solutions, today announced the promotion of Adelaine Orpin to Chief Commercial Officer (CCO), effective October 19, 2026.

In her expanded executive leadership role, Orpin will oversee Alocity's global commercial strategy, with particular emphasis on expanding relationships with value-added resellers (VARs), strategic technology partners, and enterprise customers. She will align sales channels, marketing, business development, customer technical support, and commercial operations to drive scalable revenue growth and market adoption. She will report directly to Marco Quintero, Chief Executive Officer, Chief Technology Officer, and Founder of Alocity.

The promotion reflects Alocity's commitment to strengthening its executive leadership team as the company scales the commercialization of its physical security platform.

"In just two months leading strategic accounts, Adelaine has strengthened our relationships with national channel partners, enterprise end users, and the A&E community across the Americas â the exact audiences our growth depends on," said Quintero. "As we enter our next phase of expansion, her ability to align our channel partners, sales organization, and customer experience will be instrumental in establishing Alocity as a leading force in the transformation of the physical security industry."

Orpin brings more than a decade of physical security industry experience spanning the channel, the enterprise, and the specifier community, the same audiences at the center of Alocity's commercial strategy. She joined Alocity in August 2026 as Vice President of Strategic Accounts, leading growth across the Americas with national channel partners, enterprise end users, and architecture and engineering (A&E) consultants. Previously, she spent nearly six years at Gallagher Security, most recently as Director of Sales for the US West and Canada after serving as Regional Sales Manager for the South Central US. Earlier, she spent nearly seven years at ASSA ABLOY Door Security Solutions as an Architectural Consultant and End User Specialist, advising architects, consultants, and organizations on access control and door security.

Alocity has developed an AI-powered cloud security management platform that unifies traditionally disconnected physical security technologies, including access control, video surveillance, video intercom, visitor management, and intelligent automation. Its AI operating system, featuring Aimee, an embedded conversational AI operator, enables organizations to monitor, manage, and automate security operations through a single intelligent platform.

"I am excited to take on this expanded role at such an important stage in Alocity's development," said Orpin. "We have an extraordinary opportunity to fundamentally change how organizations manage physical security through artificial intelligence, open architecture, and a unified cloud platform. My focus will be on building a world-class commercial organization, strengthening our partner ecosystem, and delivering exceptional value to our customers."

Orpin will be based at Alocity's corporate headquarters in Dania Beach, Florida, in the Greater Fort Lauderdale area.

Alocity Technologies is an innovative security technology company developing the next generation of AI-powered physical security management solutions. Our platform combines access control, AI video surveillance, video intercom, visitor management, intelligent automation, and AI agents into a unified operating system for physical spaces. Designed to integrate with existing security infrastructure, Alocity enables organizations to modernize security operations without costly replacement of legacy hardware.

For more information, visit www.alocity.com.
