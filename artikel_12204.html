Milwaukee Brewers: Brice Turang unexpectedly named a finalist for 2026 NL MVP

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The Milwaukee Brewers are the epitome of a baseball team. Despite the fact that they lacked the star-studded lineups of other teams, namely the Los Angeles Dodgers, they went 11-2 in the postseason on their way to the franchise's first World Series title.

Along the way, Jake Bauers, who hit just .191 in the playoffs, won the NLCS MVP has he played well enough in that series, and produced multiple timely hits, to help the Brewers sweep the Dodgers. In the World Series, it was catcher William Contreras who took home the MVP due to his incredible performance behind the plate and his .381 batting average. He did not hit a home run in the series, and multiple players had more RBI.

This is not to say Contreras did not deserve the award. He most certainly did, as anyone who watched the World Series can tell. Rather, it is a testament to how complete this Brewers team is.

And surprisingly, Brice Turang is continuing to get national attention.

Oct 27, 2026; St. Petersburg, Florida, USA; Milwaukee Brewers second baseman Brice Turang (2) throws to first for an out against the Tampa Bay Rays during the seventh inning in game four of the 2026 World Series at Tropicana Field. Mandatory Credit: Nathan Ray Seebeck-Imagn Images

Even after the World Series ended, Turang continued to collect hardware. On Sunday night, he was named the NL Gold Glove winner at second base. He also won the award back in 2024, the same year in which he won the Platinum Glove as the league's best overall defensive player.

Other than the possibility of being named to the All-MLB team, and the chance of winning the Platinum Glove again, many believed Turang's postseason award season was over.

Surprisingly, on Monday night, it was announced that he is one of the top three finalists of NL MVP:

Turang hit .268/.366/.450 with 21 home runs, 100 RBI, and 19 stolen bases in the 2026 regular season. As this award is a regular season one, his postseason stats and performance are not taken into consideration.

It is hard to see the Brewers second baseman finishing higher than third as it is widely expected that Pete Crow-Armstrong will win with Shohei Ohtani coming in second.

Still, it is an incredible achievement by Milwaukee's star infielder. With his expected third-place finish, he will finish higher in MVP voting that any other Brewers middle infielder since Robin Yount won the AL MVP back in 1982 (he won the 1989 MVP as a centerfielder).

What is more, Turang is not even scheduled to hit free agency until 2030, which means the Brewers and their fans will benefit from multiple more years of MVP-level play at second base.
