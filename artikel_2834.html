Ex-Giants infielder added to Yankees’ 40 after monster season in minors; 2 relievers in limbo?

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The Yankees have a bunch of free agents, so there are openings galore on their 40-man roster early into the offseason.

Intriguing middle infielder Marco Luciano filled one spot ahead of Monday's deadline to reinstate players from the 60-day injured list, exercise option years in contracts and make qualifying offers.

It's unknown if the Yankees reinstated relievers Fernando Cruz and Kervin Castro from the Yankees' 60-man IL to fill two others or designated the right-handers for assignment.

Luciano was added the roster on the Yankees website early Monday evening while Cruz and Castro were subtracted. The team announced only Luciano's addition.

With Cruz and Castro's status unknown, the Yankees officially have 31 players on their 40 — 14 pitchers and 17 position players.

Ten potential free agents officially are off the Yankees' 40-man roster, including closer David Bednar, who was extended a qualifying offer on Monday.

If Bednar accepts the $23.15-million offer by the Nov. 7 deadline, he'll be under contract for next season, delaying free agency by a year.

Their free agents are first baseman Paul Goldschmidt, second baseman Jazz Chisholm, infielder Amed Rosario, outfielder Trent Grisham and relievers Paul Blackburn, Michael Fulmer, Tim Hill, John Schreibner and Ryan Yarbrough.

The Yankees likely will attempt to re-sign Bednar if he passes on the qualifying offer and also try to keep Blackburn, who had a breakout season in a setup role.

A 25-year-old middle infielder, Luciano was added to the Yankees' 40 after putting up eye-popping offensive numbers in their system this year. A right-handed hitter, Luciano batted .293 with 27 homers and 78 RBI in 116 games at two levels.

Luciano hit .217 playing 41 games over two seasons with the Giants from 2024-25, then he changed organizations three times last winter via waiver claims. He was picked up by the Pirates on Dec. 5, then claimed by the Orioles on Jan. 7 and finally the Yankees on Jan. 22.
