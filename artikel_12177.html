Browns trade deadline: Former defender on the market; GM Andrew Berry likely has interest

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Coming off an embarrassing performance in Week 8, the Cleveland Browns have to get back on a plane for Week 9's matchup with the New Orleans Saints, who are coming off their bye week with a coaching change. With the NFL trade deadline just two days later, the outcome of Week 9 could decide which direction Cleveland GM Andrew Berry decides to go.

A 5-4 Browns team might be more likely to stay pat, while a 6-3 record could embolden one of the many trade targets being added.

Berry has spent a lot of time this offseason looking for help off the edge, which is why Cleveland is often linked to defensive ends. The Browns needed help before DE Alex Wright was lost for the season and before DE Jared Verse's combination of words and actions on the field have been called into question.

Earlier in the offseason, Berry brought in a familiar face to Berea, DE Jadeveon Clowney. In the end, Clowney chose to reunite with the Houston Texans, who were thought to have a better chance at the NFL playoffs. Given the Browns attempt to sign Clowney this offseason, it is likely Berry will have at least some interest.

As a rotational defensive end, Clowney has played 125 snaps for Houston this season contributing four QB hits, two tackles for loss, and 1.5 sacks. The Texans defense, as a whole, has been disappointing, giving up over 24 points a game after that mark was just over 17 last season.

Like Cleveland, Week 9's result could impact Houston's decision making at the trade deadline. Losing to the winless Los Angeles Chargers days before the deadline would likely lead the Texans brass to go into selling mode.
