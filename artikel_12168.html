‘It makes…’ – Why 76ers star Jaylen Brown is unhappy with his $20,000 toilet

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Philadelphia 76ers player Jaylen Brown recently showed off his Boston penthouse during a video with Architectural Digest.

Brown spent around a decade playing for the Boston Celtics but he made the move to the 76ers earlier this year.

He was the Finals MVP in 2024 when the Celtics won the championship and will now be hoping to achieve a similar feat in Philadelphia.

Although he has now left Boston, he still owns a home in the area, which includes a $20,000 toilet.

However, while the toilet cost a lot of money, Brown expressed his displeasure at it during his video with Architectural Digest, which was shared on their YouTube channel.

He mentioned how it makes ocean sounds but said it 'doesn't even work' properly.

The 76ers star stated: "What else? Oh, so this toilet sometimes p***** me off. Like, when you flush it, it makes ocean sounds. This toilet was like $20,000. Let me see if it works.

"I just heard something. This s*** doesn't even work, man."

Brown was most recently in action on Monday evening as the 76ers beat the Chicago Bulls 126-107.

He played 33 minutes, scored 25 points and provided five assists to help his side achieve a 7-1 record for the season.

Philadelphia are top of the Eastern Conference for the time being and are now set to face the Detroit Pistons on Wednesday.
