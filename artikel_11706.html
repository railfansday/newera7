FC Andorra Release Statement as CD Tenerife Announce Tragic Death of Jesus de Miguel

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

FC Andorra have now released a statement as CD Tenerife have confirmed the tragic death of striker Jesus de Miguel following a horrific collision with goalkeeper Jesus Owono.

Monday evening's Spanish Segunda Division clash was abandoned in stoppage time after De Miguel appeared to lose consciousness following the foul by Owono, who was shown a straight red card.

The scores were level at 2-2 when Tenerife cleared the ball and threatened to launch a last-gasp attack through their striker. However, the goalkeeper clattered the 30-year-old with an appalling challenge, as his knee appeared to make contact high on De Miguel's chest.

He was rushed to hospital after footage showed his teammates breaking down in tears in distressing scenes. The club then issued a worrying update that his condition was worsening at 4am, before announcing his death four hours later.

After the sad announcement on Tuesday morning, FC Andorra posted a statement of their own to honour their opponent's passing. They wrote on X (see the full post below):

"With broken hearts and devastated, we want to send our most sincere condolences to the family of Jesús de Miguel, to all his teammates, friends, and to CD Tenerife. Rest in peace."

Other clubs to pay their respects include Barcelona, who wrote: "From FC Barcelona, we deeply regret the tragic news of the passing of Jesus de Miguel, CD Tenerife player. Our thoughts and support are with his family, friends and loved ones during these difficult times."

Meanwhile, Real Madrid also posted a statement that read: "Real Madrid C. F., its president, and its Board of Directors deeply regret the passing of Jesus de Miguel, player of C. D. Tenerife.

"Real Madrid wishes to express its condolences and affection to his family, his teammates, his club, and all his loved ones. Jesus de Miguel has passed away at the age of 30. Rest in peace."

As per BBC Sport, Spanish media outlets - including Marca - reported that De Miguel suffered a cardiac arrest in the ambulance and was resuscitated by paramedics while being transported to hospital.

An exact cause of death has not been confirmed, but the player's sudden change in reaction after falling to the ground had casued immense panic on the pitch.

Spanish outlet El Partidazo de COPE say goalkeeper Owono has been left “very affected by the situation” and plans to apologise privately before making any public statement.
