Personal Finance (Not Investing) • Re: &quot;Unshackled&quot; spending post retirement anyone?

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

We spent our entire lives saving to retire. We certainly enjoyed life, but I always comparison shopped. Now that we recognize we may have too much, in addition to gifting to our kids and funding our grand's 529's, we started off tight and have evolved.

- Travel - getting suites and business class. It really enhances the trip and makes the achy bones and joints manageable.
- Car - I bought a Porsche. I love it. It was a total waste of money but I smile whenever I see or am in it. It's a beautiful car we ordered from scratch. I never would have done that 15-20 years ago.
- Clothing - always bought on sale. I still find coupon codes but shopping more at Tommy Bahama, Johnson Murphy, etc..
- Remodeling - We have enhanced our home in many ways.

We have taken the pedal off but always keeping a wary eye on waste.....!
