30-Year-Old Soccer Star Dies Hours After On-Field Collision with Opponent During Game

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Spanish soccer player Jesús de Miguel has died after colliding with an opponent during a game on Monday, Nov. 2

The 30-year-old forward, who played for CD Tenerife, was taken to the hospital in an ambulance and suffered a cardiac arrest on the journey

De Miguel's condition was initially thought to be stable but serious, but his team announced the following morning that he had died

A Spanish soccer player died in the hospital hours after colliding with an opponent during a game.

Jesús de Miguel, a forward for CD Tenerife in Spain's Segunda División, was trying to get the ball in the final minutes of a home game against FC Andorra on Monday, Nov. 2, when goalkeeper Jesús Owono crashed into him, The Guardian reported.

De Miguel, 30, lay on the ground as players pleaded for medical assistance, and Owono was shown a red card for his role in the collision. The game, which Tenerife was winning 2-0, was abandoned, the newspaper reported.

Jesús de Miguel during a game on March 23, 2024Credit: Diego Souto/Getty

Medical staff treated De Miguel on the field before he was taken to the Nuestra Señora de Candelaria University Hospital in nearby Santa Cruz de Tenerife. He suffered a cardiac arrest in the ambulance and needed to be resuscitated on the way to the hospital, per the BBC.

His condition was initially described in a statement from CD Tenerife on Monday as being stable but serious, before the team said in an update given in the early hours of Tuesday, Nov. 3, that his condition had "worsened neurologically," translated from the original Spanish.

CD Tenerife announced that De Miguel had died at about 8 a.m. local time on Tuesday morning, in another statement. "Broken with pain, with a lump in the throat and unable to believe this is true, from the Tenerife CD we communicate the tragic death," the team wrote.

"There are no words of comfort; there is absolutely nothing that can ease this feeling full of sadness. We are with his desolate family, with his loved ones, with all the Tenerife people who are crying today for this cruel outcome," the statement read.

FC Andorra paid tribute to De Miguel, writing, "We want to convey our deepest condolences to the family, friends, and teammates of Jesús de Miguel, as well as to the entire family of CD Tenerife. We are with you in these difficult moments," translated from the original Spanish.

La Liga, the top league in Spain, shared its "deep sadness" at the incident and said it would "stand in mourning" with De Miguel's loved ones in a Facebook post, while teams from across the country, including FC Barcelona, Atlético Madrid and Real Betis, paid tribute on social media.

Real Madrid shared a photo of players and staff holding a moment of silence before training on Tuesday morning in memory of Madrid-born De Miguel.

De Miguel joined Tenerife in 2025 from CD Castellón. After beginning his career with Leganés, he played for teams including Internacional Madrid, Fuenlabrada, Unión Adarve, Navalcarnero and Unionistas, the BBC reported.

Leganés also held a moment of silence before training, the team wrote on Facebook.

"In mourning for the passing of Jesús De Miguel, the Castellón CD has suspended today's training," Castellón said. "Out of respect for his memory, the club will not make any further posts on its social media today. Rest in peace, Demi."

The Royal Spanish Football Federation announced in a statement that all games scheduled for Tuesday have been postponed as a "sign of respect and solidarity," with a new schedule set to arrive in the coming days.

Per Spanish outlet ABC, De Miguel is survived by his wife María and their 16-month-old daughter Carla.
