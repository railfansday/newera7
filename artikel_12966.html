Investing - Theory, News &amp; General • Re: When the market tanks, what do you plan to do in retirement?

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

If stocks drop 20%, and look like they will continue to go down, I will immediately sell all my stocks, and plan to buy back in at the bottom. It seems like the only sensible thing to do. I'd avoid huge losses, and potentially get huge gains. Kidding.

I will rebalance according to plan. My first rebalance will occur after about a 20% drop. Then again if it drops another 10%. And so on. The only change in my plan since retirement is to make sure I keep at least 10 years in "safe" assets. I don't want to rebalance into a rabbit hole. I have not calculated how much of a drop that would take, since I don't know when, or if, it will occur. Hopefully 10 years is enough time for the market to recover/normalize.

I know a 50% (or more) drop in stocks is possible, but that does not mean it has to happen during my lifetime. I do not consider it a foregone conclusion. Just a possibility.

We have no stocks in after tax accounts, just a savings account, so no tax loss harvesting possible.
We already do Roth conversions every year. I would make those conversions part of my rebalancing plan.

I would not consciously change our spending during the first year of this mythical market tank. But we might subconsciously reduce spending. It might be hard not to. I use an ABW based withdrawal method. Depending on assumptions that gives a range of reasonable spending. So far we spend toward the lower end of that reasonable spending range. If the market went down 50% (or more), and stayed there, eventually we would almost have cut back on spending. I hope that day never comes, but I know it could.
