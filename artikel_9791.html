I Built a Free AI Tools Directory Where a Footer Badge Buys Dofollow (Skipping It Keeps You Listed)

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Most AI directories force a binary choice: pay for a slot, or hand over a permanent backlink before you get indexed. I wanted a third path for my own AI products—and for other indie tools that are still early.

So I shipped FindAIForMe: a free / freemium AI tools hub with open submissions, a public review queue, and a badge check that upgrades your official-site link from nofollow to dofollow. Skip the badge and you stay listed. That last part is the product decision I care about most as a builder.

This is how the rules work on the live site today—not a pitch deck.

I needed a place to park my own tools with honest outbound SEO, then opened submissions so the catalog was not just my portfolio. The homepage leads with a Free AI tools section, then category cards (chat, image, video, coding, automation, and more). The catalog is in the ~10k+ tools range. There is also a public AI Rank board for already-trafficked AI sites (filters for category, growth, and related signals—always re-check the live page).

If you are shipping an AI side project, that filter is the whole point: the index stays useful instead of turning into “every SaaS with a chatbot widget.”

Paid listings and owner / operator sites are exempt from the badge check. Free sites that have not hung a badge show a pending-badge state with embed code and a re-check control—they are not silently deleted.

The 30-day free review window is deliberate. Instant auto-approve sounds nice until the directory fills with parked domains. A short queue is the cost of keeping the catalog readable.

The submit flow asks for logo URL, one-line pitch, category, and official site. After approval you get a public detail page under /tools/ (for example /tools/n8n). Detail pages summarize positioning, pricing tag, category, and light selection notes. They are summaries—not substitutes for vendor docs.

That is the product bet: discovery indexing stays open for free tools; dofollow juice is reserved for sites that keep a durable, verifiable referral. Hostages-or-nothing directories train people to strip badges the day after approval. Keeping unbadged free listings live (nofollow) makes the bargain feel less extractive—and still gives badge partners a clear upgrade path.
