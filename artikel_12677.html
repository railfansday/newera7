Verito Announces TaxDome Verified Host Provider Status

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The designation gives firms hosted on Verito priority support and centrally managed updates for their TaxDome desktop application, paired with VeritShield WISP, Verito's Written Information Security Plan service.

ORLANDO, Fla., Nov. 3, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Verito, a cloud hosting and managed IT provider built exclusively for tax and accounting firms, and TaxDome, the practice management platform used by tax and accounting firms to run client work end to end, today announced a new partnership naming Verito a TaxDome Verified Host Provider, a status TaxDome created for hosting providers it vets against its own service standards.

What TaxDome Verified Host Provider Status Delivers

Verito & TaxDome announce official partnership with TaxDome Verified Host Provider status

TaxDome Verified Host Provider TaxDome is now putting its name behind Verito's hosting for the firms that run TaxDome on it.

That status is tied to specific, ongoing standards, not a one-time check: a 24-hour response commitment on any issue TaxDome submits, advance notice of changes that could affect performance or security, and hosting practices that exceed industry standards for access control, logging, and customer-data handling.

Priority support. Verito maintains a designated technical contact and dedicated support channel for TaxDome, with a 24-hour initial response guaranteed on any issue TaxDome submits. That sits on top of Verito's support model for firms: sub-60-second live response and technicians trained on the professional tax and accounting applications firms run alongside TaxDome.

Centrally managed updates. Verito hosts TaxDome's desktop application on private, dedicated servers and manages update deployment directly. Any change either company expects could affect performance, security, or integrations gets advance written notice under the agreement, so updates roll out on a controlled schedule.

Written Information Security Plan (WISP) at a discount. Firms that come to Verito through TaxDome receive a discount on VeritShield WISP for a Written Information Security Plan scoped to how the firm actually works: where client documents land, who can reach them, how they move, and what happens when someone leaves, with unlimited revisions throughout the year.

TaxDome sits on the same private server as the rest of the firm's software, including Drake, UltraTax, Lacerte, ProSeries, CCH ProSystem fx, and QuickBooks, so client files, source documents, and the tax applications all open in one place.

The result: TaxDome firms get one verified host, priority support, and a discounted path to compliance. Firm should visit Verito's TaxDome partner page to learn more.

When a hosting provider is slow to deploy a desktop application update, integrations break in the middle of filing season. Documents stop syncing. E-signatures stall. Staff wait on a host's update queue while deadlines do not move.

Most firms satisfy their IRS Publication 4557 and FTC Safeguards Rule WISP obligation with a downloadable template that never names the software the firm actually uses. A plan that does not describe the real workflow does not describe the real risk. Verito's WISP service fills this gap, taking the guess-work out of compliance paperwork.

"At TaxDome, we believe accounting firms should have the freedom to choose the best technology for their business without sacrificing security, performance, or simplicity," said Petar Iliev, Chief Revenue Officer at TaxDome. "Our partnership with Verito is another step toward building a more connected ecosystem where technology providers work together to remove complexity for firms. By combining TaxDome's practice management platform with Verito's hosting expertise, we're helping firms spend less time managing technology and more time serving their clients and growing their businesses."
