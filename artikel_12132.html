Page's hat trick powers Bay soccer past Arnold in Hammond's return

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

PANAMA CITY BEACH, Fla. — Bay High boys soccer senior forward Dushawn Page scored a hat trick Monday, Nov. 2 to lead the Tornadoes to a 4-1 road victory over Arnold at Mike Gavlak Stadium. The victory spoiled the Marlins' home opener, but was also the scene of an emotional return for Tornadoes head coach Jonathan Hammond.

Hammond, who spent a decade leading Arnold's soccer program before taking over at Bay High, returned to the field where he helped build one of Florida's premier high school programs.

Junior Jhampool Parra opened the scoring before Page took over, finding the back of the net twice in the first half to give Bay High a commanding 3-0 advantage at the break.

Arnold got on the scoreboard when defender Josh Moore faked out a couple of defenders and found the net, but Page completed his hat trick with a cheeky chip of the ball over the Arnold goalkeeper to secure the 4-1 win.

Hammond praised Page's performance, particularly his ability to deliver when the attack was not at its best.

"We have three guys up top, and our other two guys were off tonight," Hammond said. "You can't stop all three of them, and Dushy played lights out tonight."

Hammond said Page's development has grown across the years.

"He's such a special player, and he's going to do big things this year," Hammond said. "Just a great player and a joy to be around."

Despite the four-goal performance, Hammond said the Tornadoes have plenty to improve on after a victory that did not meet his expectations for the team's overall performance.

"We thought we were going to be more dominant, and we weren't because of our own mistakes," Hammond said. "It's good that we have something to go out and look at and fix."

The match also provided an opportunity for Hammond to see his starting group play the full 80 minutes, something he said would benefit the team as the season progresses.

For Hammond, the result carried significance beyond the scoreboard.

During his 10 seasons at Arnold, Hammond compiled a 187-23-15 record, including a 75-game unbeaten streak at Mike Gavlak Stadium. His teams captured a MaxPreps national championship in 2024, three state championships (2021, 2024 and 2025), five regional titles, nine district championships and 10 county titles while making five state tournament appearances.

The transition to Bay High has given Hammond an opportunity to apply what he learned during his time with the Marlins while establishing a new standard for the Tornadoes.

"I learned so much in my time here at Arnold that when I made the transition, I got to sit down and think about, ok, I'm building a new program. What do I want that to look like?" Hammond said.

He added that the standards at Bay are even higher as he works to build the program.

"It's going to take time. I'm not worried," Hammond said. "Once this team starts clicking — you don't want to be clicking at the beginning of the year. You want to be clicking at the end of the year."

Hammond said he was ready to put the return to Arnold behind him and turn his attention toward the remainder of the season, while also acknowledging the possibility of another meeting in the district playoffs.

"For me, I wanted to kind of get it over with, rip the Band-Aid off, put it behind us and focus on the rest of the season," Hammond said.

He also credited Arnold for its performance and expressed appreciation for Moore's goal, noting the defender plays club soccer with several Bay High players.

"I'm happy for him that he got a goal; that's going to mean a lot to him, scoring against a lot of his friends," Hammond said.

With the first meeting behind them, Hammond is looking forward to seeing how both programs develop before a potential postseason rematch.

"It's going to be fun. It's going to be a fun ride," Hammond said. "I look forward to seeing Arnold in the playoffs, the district playoffs, and seeing how that match goes, how they've grown and then how we've grown."

Bay High (1-0) hosts West Florida on Frida Nov. 6. Arnold (0-1) hosts Mosley on Friday, Nov. 6.
