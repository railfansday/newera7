Avalanche-Blackhawks Live Update Thread

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Artturi Lehkonen speaking to the media following their 5-2 win over Calgary on Halloween.

It was an unfortunate start for the Avalanche, as Blackhawks defenseman Wyatt Kaiser was credited with a goal after his point shot deflected off Cale Makar and past Mackenzie Blackwood, who was unable to track the puck in time.

Colorado then earned a power play after Ethan Del Mastro was called for interference. However, the Avalanche failed to capitalize, and their road struggles continued as they fell behind 2-0 at the 7:05 mark. After a costly turnover, Connor Bedard set up Roman Kantserov for a one-timer that beat Blackwood over the glove.
