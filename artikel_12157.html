‘I don’t give a…’ – Caleb Williams ruthlessly mocking Seattle Seahawks is going viral

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The Chicago Bears travelled to Seattle and took the win from the reigning Super Bowl champions and Caleb Williams had no time for the 'Dark Side' defense.

Williams put up almost 250 yards on the Seahawks on Monday night which is a rare feat for any quarterback and it was apparent that the 24-year-old was pumped up for the game. After the Bears took the lead early on, Williams expressed his passion on the sideline.

It has been a difficult season for Williams after suffering his injury which kept him out for a number of weeks. Beating a team like the Seahawks was just what the Bears needed to solidify their position as serious contenders this season.

The Seahawks' defense has been listed by many as the best in the league and Williams was keen to remind everyone just what he and his offense were able to do to the Dark Side in Seattle's own home.

Monday night's game saw the Bears score more points on the Seahawks since the Los Angeles Rams back in December of last year. Make no mistake about it, this was a real statement of a performance from Williams and the Bears.

"I don't give a f*** who they are!" Williams was spotted shouting on the Bears' sideline after putting up their first touchdown of the game.

Seattle's Dark Side defense witnessed the Bears put together an 80-yard scoring drive which lasted over eight minutes. The Bears' offense has certainly provided a lot of teams issues under Ben Johnson, but this was perhaps the biggest bit of confirmation for any doubters across the league.

The Bears put up 363 yards of total offense on Seattle and completely dominated the Seahawks when it came to third-down conversion. With over 35 minutes of possession, this was a night to remember for the Bears' offense.

It is also a performance that they will be keen to build on after they established themselves fully in the race for the number one seed in the NFC.
