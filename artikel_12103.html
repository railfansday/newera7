Arsenal are in the race to sign Premier League striker

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Mikel Arteta is preparing to strengthen Arsenal's attacking options during the January transfer window as the Gunners pursue another Premier League title and Champions League success.

According to The Mirror, Arteta has identified the recruitment of a top-class forward as his leading priority, with Bournemouth's Junior Kroupi and Juventus winger Kenan Yildiz among the players attracting interest.

Arsenal's interest in Kroupi comes amid growing competition for the Bournemouth forward, with Paris Saint-Germain and Bayern Munich also monitoring his situation.

Bournemouth are reluctant to sanction a January departure, potentially complicating Arsenal's plans.

Arteta reportedly wants a versatile attacker capable of operating across several positions, although strengthening the left flank remains particularly important.

Arsenal previously explored moves for Morgan Rogers and Vinicius Junior during the summer without securing either player.

With the Gunners three points clear at the Premier League summit, additional attacking depth could prove crucial during the second half of the campaign.

Arsenal travel to Slavia Prague on Wednesday as they continue their pursuit of European honours.

Sixteen-year-old Max Dowman could feature despite serving a three-match domestic suspension following his dismissal against Fleetwood.

Arteta previously warned the youngster that "we are not in Disneyland", emphasising the standards expected at the highest level.

However, Dowman remains available for European competition and could start or feature from the bench.

Arsenal also face Hull City on Saturday, with Arteta expected to rotate following the victory over Liverpool.

Meanwhile, William Saliba is progressing towards a return after his back injury. The defender has resumed grass training and awaits medical clearance before rejoining the first-team squad.

For Arteta, January recruitment could prove decisive as Arsenal attempt to sustain their challenge across multiple competitions.

Arsenal's reported intention to strengthen in January should excite supporters who recognise the opportunity facing Mikel Arteta this season.

Being three points clear at the top of the Premier League is encouraging, particularly when the club also harbour serious ambitions of winning the Champions League.

However, maintaining that position requires sufficient attacking quality throughout a demanding campaign.

Junior Kroupi is an intriguing target, although Bournemouth's reluctance to sell suggests negotiations could prove difficult.

Kenan Yildiz would also represent an exciting addition, particularly given Arsenal's desire for greater versatility across their forward line.

My concern is whether Arsenal can secure the right player without allowing the urgency of January recruitment to influence their judgement.

Arteta needs someone capable of making an immediate contribution. Signing a forward simply to increase numbers would offer limited value.

There is also an encouraging opportunity for Max Dowman to continue his development. His suspension provides a reminder of the challenges facing young footballers, although his European availability offers another chance to impress.

Saliba's anticipated return could prove equally important, particularly during a congested fixture schedule.

Arsenal have placed themselves in a promising position. Supporters will expect the club's recruitment team to recognise what is at stake.

A successful January window could significantly strengthen their prospects of securing major silverware.
