Peyton Manning wowed by what he spotted the Bears doing to Seattle’s defence

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

The Chicago Bears confirmed their status as one of the true NFC contenders this season with their impressive performance on offense against the Seattle Seahawks.

Ben Johnson's offense against Mike Macdonald's defense promised to be one of the best matchups of the 2026 season, and it did not disappoint. The Bears have proven themselves to be the real deal in what is one of the best victories of Johnson's tenure in Chicago.

Although everyone knew that Chicago had a brilliant offensive unit, beating the reigning Super Bowl champions – who have one of the best defenses – in the manner they did on Monday night was a real statement to the rest of the NFC.

Johnson pulled out all of the stops at Lumen Field to get the better of the 'Dark Side' defense and Peyton Manning pointed out one of the tricks the Bears' offense pulled off.

Seattle put on a defensive masterclass last week against the Kansas City Chiefs as they became the first team ever to prevent Patrick Mahomes from entering the red zone. However, Monday night against the Bears was a totally different story.

"Watch the [Bears' offensive] linemen here. Look at them, they're going, 'Go, go, go! On the ball, on the ball! Same play!' said Manning.

"They're all doing it on purpose, and the Seattle D-linemen are hearing that. They're going, 'Hey, it's the same play.' Watch the linebackers come up the middle. Look at them. These guys are all future movie stars, Eli: they can block, and they can act."

It was just another example of the tricks Johnson has up his sleeve when it comes to getting the absolute best out of his offense. The Bears had the Seahawks defense completely fooled on this play, leading to the opening touchdown of the game.
