Florida's Jon Sumrall speaks on Georiga loss, looks ahead to Oklahoma

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Florida football is coming off an embarrassing Week 9 loss to Georgia in the annual rivalry game, which dropped the team to 5-3. With four games left on the schedule and Oklahoma coming to town next weekend, head coach Jon Sumrall spoke with the media on Monday about the challenges his team faces as it heads into the home stretch.

Among the topics discussed were the mistake both he and his team made against the Bulldogs and the corrective measures he is taking to resolve them, as well as the injury issues facing several players — including Vernell Brown III and Duke Clark — plus the situation at the quarterback position as well as a look ahead at the Sooners.

Below is a look at everything Sumrall said to the media on Monday.
