Personal Finance (Not Investing) • Re: Teaching children about high deductible health plans

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

It’s my thinking that with most things in life you will get punished financially for chronically making decisions that lead to more dependency on others or institutions. I think it’s over the general public’s head when one starts talking about “triple tax advantaged”, investing instead of using HSA money (but saving big ticket receipts just in case) and paying out of pocket for any services you can afford. When the average Joe doesn’t have an emergency fund to cover $1000 expense but has a brand new truck that costs a years of wages financed at 8-10% and his ol lady has the latest SUV that’s a lease….such thoughts are totally irrelevant. With my kids , I try to tell them/lead by example to NOT be normal because normal is broke, sick, obese, and indentured to the system…not fun positions to be in. If only I was given this advice as a young adult, it wouldn’t taken me well over a decade as an adult of being normal and untold amounts of money to finally figure it out.
