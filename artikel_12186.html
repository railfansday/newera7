Spanish Soccer Player Dies a Day After Video of Horrific Mid-Game Collision Went Viral

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

A Spanish soccer player died after a horrific collision with an opposing goalkeeper during a match that went viral and caused the game to be called as distressed teammates desperately called for medical assistance.

Jesús de Miguel, 30, a striker for CD Tenerife, died Tuesday morning after suffering a cardiac arrest following a violent challenge by FC Andorra goalkeeper Jesús Owono during Monday's Spanish second-division clash.

The incident unfolded deep into stoppage time at Tenerife's Estadio Heliodoro Rodríguez López, when Owono charged out of his penalty area toward the halfway line to challenge De Miguel for a long ball.

The goalkeeper collided with the striker, appearing to strike him in the chest with his knee before walking away.

De Miguel immediately collapsed to the ground, prompting players from both teams to urgently summon medical staff. Owono was immediately shown a red card, the third dismissal of an already heated encounter, before the referee suspended the match with the score tied at 2-2.

As footage of the hit went viral online, garnering millions of views, De Miguel was rushed to a hospital where he was admitted to intensive care with serious injuries.

His club initially reported that he was "stable despite the severity of his condition" late Monday, before revealing in an overnight update that his condition had "worsened neurologically".

On Tuesday, Tenerife confirmed the striker had died at 8 a.m. local time.

"Heartbroken, with a lump in our throats and unable to believe that this is true," the club said in a statement announcing his death. "There are no words of comfort; there is absolutely nothing that can ease this overwhelming sense of sadness. We stand with his devastated family, with his loved ones, and with the entire Tenerife footballing community, who today mourn this cruel turn of events."

Andorra, the club owned by former Barcelona defender Gerard Piqué, also issued a tribute on X: "With broken hearts and devastated, we want to send our most sincere condolences to the family of Jesus de Miguel, to all his teammates, friends, and to CD Tenerife. Rest in peace."

After news broke of de Miguel's passing, Spain's soccer federation postponed scheduled domestic cup matches, and UEFA, the European football federation, announced moments of silence ahead of that week's slate of European matches.
