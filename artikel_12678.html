Asymbl Launches My Day in Recruiter Suite, Prioritizing Recruiters' Highest-Value Work First

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

My Day is now generally available in every Recruiter Suite edition at no additional cost.

AUSTIN, Texas, Nov. 3, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Asymbl, The Workforce Orchestration Companyâ¢, today announced the general availability of My Day, a new feature in Recruiter Suite that organizes a recruiter's day around the work with the highest impact, determined by a firm, and shows why each item matters. My Day is included in every Recruiter Suite edition at no additional cost.

Recruiters are the revenue engine of a staffing firm. They also spend most of their day on admin work spread across disconnected screens. Priorities have lived in their heads, on sticky notes, and inside spreadsheets. My Day pulls that into one place, so recruiters spend less time deciding where to start and more time building talent relationships and placing the right people faster.

"Work should be more productive, more predictable, and more meaningful. That only happens when software moves the outcomes our customers are chasing, not just the activity around them. My Day gives recruiters their time back, and gives staffing leaders confidence that the right work is getting done first," said Greg Symons, President and Founder of Asymbl.
