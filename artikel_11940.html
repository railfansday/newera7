Derby County to assess 31-year-old ahead of Norwich City game

Tanggal: 2026-11-03

Derby County need to make checks on the wing-back as they prepare to take on the Canaries. DCFC left him out of their last outing against Millwall at The Den due to an issue with his back.

Ward, 31, has made 11 appearances for the Rams in all competitions so far this season, 10 of which have come in the Championship. He is under contract at Pride Park until June 2027 meaning he has entered the final 12 months of his deal.

In this latest update regarding his condition, his head coach Eustace has said, as per a report by DerbyshireLive: “He’s going to be touch and go for tomorrow night for sure. He had a back spasm on Friday unfortunately just prior to training so we obviously had to change things around. Hopefully he won’t be too bad. We assessed him today, he was a little bit better so let’s see for tomorrow.”

Derby County remain 23rd in the table following their 4-1 loss away at Millwall last time out and they are in the bottom three along with Preston North End and Burnley. They are four points from safety behind Watford.

Derby County won’t want to take any risks with Ward. DCFC will only play him against Norwich City if he is 100% fit.

Max Johnston came in for him against Millwall last Saturday. The 22-year-old will be ready to step up again if needed against the Canaries.

Ward, who is from Chelmsford, has been on the books of the Rams since joining them back in 2023. He has since been a good servant to the club and has played 86 games.

The ex-England C international is an experienced operator in the EFL. Prior to his switch to Derby County, he had spells at Brighton and Hove Albion, Woking and Peterborough United.

Derby County manager Eustace didn’t give a definitive answer on whether Ward will feature against Norwich City. DCFC’s boss didn’t want to give too much away.

The Rams need to return to winning ways against the Canaries. They have won only once so far this term and have been struggling for results.

Eustace is under pressure to turn things around. Another loss on Tuesday will turn up the heat on him even more.

Derby County have a decision to make about Ward’s long-term future amid his uncertain contract situation. They risk losing him for free when this season ends.

It remains to be seen at this stage whether they will hand him an extension. In the meantime, he will be focused on getting back to full fitness.
